<div class="container faq">
	<div class="row">
		<div class="col-lg-10 mx-auto">

			<div class="faq-item">
				<h3 class="faq-question">What does a bond originator do?</h3>
				<div class="faq-answer">
					<p>A bond originator submits your home-loan application to several banks on your behalf, compares the quotes they return, and helps you negotiate a competitive interest rate. A bond originator is not a lender — we act as your intermediary with the banks and guide you through the paperwork and the process from application to registration.</p>
				</div>
			</div>

			<div class="faq-item">
				<h3 class="faq-question">What is pre-qualification and why does it matter?</h3>
				<div class="faq-answer">
					<p>Pre-qualification is an assessment of your income, expenses and existing debt to indicate how much you may be able to borrow. It helps you shop for a home within a realistic price range so you don't waste time viewing properties outside your budget. Pre-qualification is an indication only — the final amount, rate and approval remain subject to the selected bank's full assessment and the signed purchase agreement.</p>
				</div>
			</div>

			<div class="faq-item">
				<h3 class="faq-question">What documents are needed for a home loan?</h3>
				<div class="faq-answer">
					<p>Most banks ask for a copy of your ID, proof of income (recent payslips and IRP5, or, for the self-employed, financial statements and tax returns), three to six months of bank statements, proof of residence, and the signed offer to purchase once you have one. Having these ready helps avoid delays.</p>
				</div>
			</div>

			<div class="faq-item">
				<h3 class="faq-question">How long does bond approval take?</h3>
				<div class="faq-answer">
					<p>Timelines vary by bank and by how complete your application is. A straightforward application with all documents submitted can often be assessed within a few working days, while more complex cases may take a few weeks. Submitting complete information early is the best way to keep things moving.</p>
				</div>
			</div>

						<div class="faq-item">
				<h3 class="faq-question">Can self-employed applicants qualify for a home loan?</h3>
				<div class="faq-answer">
					<p>Yes. Self-employed buyers can qualify, usually with additional documentation such as personal and business financial statements, SARS tax returns and bank statements. Lenders assess affordability in the same way, so clear, well-organised records make the process smoother.</p>
				</div>
			</div>

			<div class="faq-item">
				<h3 class="faq-question">Can I get a home loan with a low deposit?</h3>
				<div class="faq-answer">
					<p>The standard minimum deposit is typically 10% of the property value, though some banks may accept lower amounts. A larger deposit reduces your monthly repayment and may improve your loan terms. Use our <a href="/deposit-savings-calculator">deposit savings calculator</a> to estimate how much you need to save.</p>
				</div>
			</div>

			<div class="faq-item">
				<h3 class="faq-question">Can I refinance or switch my existing home loan?</h3>
				<div class="faq-answer">
					<p>Yes. If you already have a bond, it is often worth reviewing whether switching lenders or refinancing could lower your monthly repayment. A bond originator can request quotes from other banks to compare. Any saving depends on current rates, your profile and the bank's assessment.</p>
				</div>
			</div>

			<div class="faq-item">
				<h3 class="faq-question">Does making an enquiry affect my credit score?</h3>
				<div class="faq-answer">
					<p>An initial, obligation-free conversation with us does not affect your credit score. A formal credit enquiry is only triggered when a lender runs a credit check as part of an actual application.</p>
				</div>
			</div>

			<div class="faq-item">
				<h3 class="faq-question">Are there costs to use a bond originator?</h3>
				<div class="faq-answer">
					<p>For standard home loans, bond originator commission is generally paid by the lending bank rather than by you, but it is always best to confirm the specific arrangement with us before proceeding. We are happy to explain any costs clearly.</p>
				</div>
			</div>

			<div class="faq-item">
				<h3 class="faq-question">How much home loan can I qualify for?</h3>
				<div class="faq-answer">
					<p>Banks typically lend up to around 80–90% of the property value (or purchase price). Your exact affordability depends on your income, expenses, existing debts, credit profile and the current interest rate. Use our <a href="/affordability-calculator">affordability calculator</a> for an estimate.</p>
				</div>
			</div>

			<div class="faq-item">
				<h3 class="faq-question">What is the difference between pre-qualification and final approval?</h3>
				<div class="faq-answer">
					<p>Pre-qualification is a preliminary estimate based on the information you provide. Final approval requires a full application, credit check, income verification and property assessment by the bank. We help you through both stages and keep you informed along the way.</p>
				</div>
			</div>

			<div class="faq-disclaimer">
				<p>This information is general guidance only. Approval, interest rates and lending decisions are subject to each bank's assessment and their specific criteria. For advice tailored to your situation, please <a href="/contact">contact us</a>.</p>
			</div>
