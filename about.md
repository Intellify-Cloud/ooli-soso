			<p>oobalink Northern Suburbs is a Cape Town-based bond originator and home-loan advisory practice. We help property buyers and homeowners across the Western Cape — and nationally — compare home-loan quotes from South Africa's major banks and guide them from pre-qualification through to registration.</p>

			<h3>Our Story</h3>
			<p>The business was established in 2004 and is headed by Antonella Werth, a partner in the oobalink group of originators. Antonella has many years of experience in home finance and has grown the practice into a well-respected player in the bond origination market.</p>

			<h3>What We Do</h3>
			<p>Because home loans are our business and our focus is getting your bond approved, we:</p>
			<ul>
				<li>Prequalify you before you start house hunting</li>
				<li>Assist you in completing one set of application forms which we submit to multiple banks, including your own</li>
				<li>Negotiate with the banks to help you secure the best possible deal and interest rate</li>
				<li>Keep you informed throughout the home-loan process</li>
				<li>Make the bond application process as easy and hassle-free as possible</li>
			</ul>

			<h3>Our Service Area</h3>
			<p>We are based in Durbanville Hills, Northern Suburbs, Cape Town, and serve clients across the Western Cape and nationally.</p>
